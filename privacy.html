Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

The app is used only by its owner. When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the open ID of the owner's account with the video.upload permission.

  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.
  • Videos are sent only to TikTok servers through the official Content Posting API.
  • The app collects no data about other people and uses no analytics or advertising.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings, and the stored token is then deleted.
